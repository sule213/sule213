# Dr Suleiman Salihu Mustafa

**Postdoctoral Research Associate in Computer Vision**
Department of Clinical Neurosciences, University of Cambridge

I build and evaluate artificial intelligence for medical imaging. My current work applies foundation models to 3D scans, with a focus on PET/CT segmentation and machine learning for tuberculosis (TB) treatment outcome prediction.

## Research interests

- Foundation models for 3D medical imaging
- Medical image segmentation, including PET/CT
- Machine learning for treatment outcome prediction, including class imbalance and ensemble methods
- Explainable AI and model interpretation for clinical imaging
- Model calibration, uncertainty and validation for safe clinical use
- Self-supervised and representation learning for longitudinal imaging

## Current work

- **Merlin foundation model evaluation:** deploying and evaluating Merlin, a foundation model for 3D medical imaging, fully offline inside a secure high-performance computing environment. The pipeline covers zero-shot scoring, linear probing on frozen embeddings, and Grad-CAM interpretation for TB classification from CT scans.
- **PredicTB:** machine learning for TB treatment outcome prediction, including a three-bag ensemble meta-learning framework designed to correct for severe class imbalance.
- **PET/CT segmentation and analysis:** segmentation and image-omics pipelines for large scale PET/CT datasets.
- **Supervision:** co-supervising MPhil students on the MPhil in Translating Medical Device and Advanced Therapy Research, with projects in explainability, uncertainty, self-supervision, agentic AI for feature extraction, and PET/CT segmentation.
